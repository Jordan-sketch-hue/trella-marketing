# Trella Marketing Consultant — Brand Identity

Brand system derived directly from the client's real logo (`tm.jpg`). Colors below were sampled
pixel-by-pixel from the logo artwork, not approximated.

## Logo

The mark is a dynamic italic **TM** monogram — an ultramarine **T** interlocked with a red **M** —
beside the **trella MARKETING Consultant** wordmark.

| Asset | File | Use |
|------|------|-----|
| Full lockup (transparent) | `public/logo-transparent.png` | Headers, light surfaces |
| Full lockup (white bg) | `public/logo.png` | Where a solid plate is wanted |
| Monogram (transparent) | `public/monogram-transparent.png` | App icon, compact marks, dark surfaces |
| Monogram (white bg) | `public/monogram.png` | Favicon / icon source |
| Original (untouched) | `public/logo.jpg` | Master / reference |
| Favicons | `src/app/icon.png`, `src/app/apple-icon.png` | Browser + iOS |

**Usage**
- On **light** backgrounds: full colour lockup.
- On **dark** backgrounds: use the `<Logo onDark />` variant — the colour logo sits inside a white chip (the logo is multi-colour and should never be recoloured).
- In tight spaces / app icons: the **TM monogram** alone.
- Don't stretch, rotate, recolour, or add effects beyond the logo's own built-in depth.

Used on the footer, stat bands, and CTAs.

## Typography

- **Display / headings:** Sora (500–800), tight tracking (`-0.02em`).
- **Body / UI:** Inter (400–700).

Loaded via Google Fonts in `src/app/layout.tsx`; exposed as `--font-display` and `--font-sans`.

## Voice

Confident, plain-spoken, results-first. We talk about outcomes (reservations, leads, revenue,
ROAS) — not jargon. Tagline: **"Marketing that moves the needle."**
